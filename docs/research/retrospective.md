# Retrospective sweep tracker

<!-- retrospective: last-swept=2026-09-14 logs-considered=215 -->

Living ledger of `/promoting-work-log-insights` sweeps — one row per work log,
recording whether it has been swept and what came out of it. Updated **in
place** on every run, the same convention
[`harness-refresh.md`](harness-refresh.md) follows; `docs/research/README.md`'s
index links here rather than listing dated copies.

`check:retrospective` (ADR-0084) reads only the machine-readable header
comment above. `last-swept=unset` is the never-swept state and is treated the
same as "past the staleness threshold" — it warns immediately rather than
letting a scaffolded-but-empty tracker read as fresh. The gate also compares
`logs-considered` against the live count of `docs/logs/*.md` and warns once
the backlog reaches 5 logs, the cadence `/writing-work-logs` documents and
that, before this tracker, nothing polled.

**The backlog is not currently zero.** The first real sweep (2026-09-01)
cleared the 14-log forward window; a follow-up pass the same day cleared the
remaining 13 older logs (2026-06-29 → 2026-08-16). A same-day sweep then
picked up the one log (`2026-09-01-v3-secrets-delivery.md`) that landed after
the backlog-clearing pass, so `logs-considered` tracked the live count
exactly at that point. The gate then went quiet on the count arm until the
next 5-log cadence produced a gap — that is the mechanism working. It
produced exactly that gap on 2026-09-02: nine logs accumulated in a single
day's work, the gate warned at the cadence, and the sweep that date cleared
them back to zero. It produced the same gap again on 2026-09-03 — nine more

**A row's outcome is not a per-insight receipt.** Read the source log's bullets,
not this table, when deciding whether a insight is already promoted:
`2026-08-20-f23-reviewable-slice-discipline.md` carries five insights of which
two were stamped, yet its row reads a flat `promoted`. The 2026-09-03 sweep
recovered an unpromoted insight from that log (`*/` closing a block comment)
only because Step 2's filter works at bullet level. A `promoted` row means
"this log has been swept", never "every insight in it landed somewhere".

## Why a ledger and not just the in-log marker

Promoted insights have always been stamped back into their source log
(`_(promoted → .claude/rules/tests.md)_`). That marker records a **positive**
outcome and nothing else, so it cannot distinguish the two states a sweep most
needs to tell apart:

- a log that was **read and found to carry nothing durable** — correctly
  swept, correctly produced no edit; and
- a log that was **never read at all**.

From outside, both look identical: no marker. That is why 27 of 112 logs
could not be accounted for before this tracker existed. The
`no-durable-insight` outcome is the row that makes the difference expressible,
and the first sweep used it immediately: of the 14 logs it read in full, **6
produced a promotion and 8 did not** — and those 8 are now recorded as read,
each with the filter that rejected it, rather than left indistinguishable from
the 13 nobody has opened.

## Outcomes

| Outcome              | Meaning                                                                                                        |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `promoted`           | Swept; at least one insight reached `.claude/rules/`, `.claude/agents/`, a skill, or a `.claude/hooks/` guard. |
| `no-durable-insight` | Swept in full; nothing cleared the Step 2 filters. A real result, not a gap.                                   |
| `deferred`           | Swept; a candidate was found but held back, with the reason in the notes.                                      |
| `not-yet-swept`      | Backfilled state — never read by a sweep. The backlog this tracker counts.                                     |

## Backfill note

Rows marked `promoted` with a `Swept` date **before 2026-09-01** are derived
from provenance markers already in the logs, not from a sweep run against this
tracker; their date is the log's own, which is when the promotion was stamped.
Rows dated 2026-09-01 come from the first real sweep. The 13 remaining
`not-yet-swept` rows are the genuine backlog. No log was retroactively marked
`no-durable-insight` during the backfill — that outcome is only ever recorded
by a sweep that actually read the log, and inventing it would recreate exactly
the ambiguity this file exists to remove. Every `no-durable-insight` row below
was earned by a sweep reading that log in full.
